Privacy Shield — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Privacy Shield is a browser extension that warns you before you give your phone number to a website that may sell it or pass it to other companies who will contact you. This page explains what the extension does and does not do with your information.

What the extension accesses

  • Page content on sites you visit. The extension reads the current page's HTML, locally in your browser, to detect form fields that ask for a phone number and to find the link to that site's own privacy policy. Nothing is sent anywhere unless a phone-number field is found.
  • When a phone-number field is found: the extension sends two things to the Privacy Shield backend: the domain name of the page (e.g. example.com) and the address of the privacy policy link on that page. That's all — never the page itself and never anything you type.

What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not collect, store, or transmit the actual information you type into forms (your phone number, email address, name, etc.).
  • It does not track your browsing history. Only the domain and privacy policy address of a page with a phone-number field are sent, and only to rate that site.
  • It does not run any advertising or third-party analytics.

Third parties contacted

  • The Privacy Shield backend service (a Cloudflare Worker operated by the developer) — receives the domain and privacy policy address described above. The backend itself downloads that public privacy policy page from the website, rates it, and caches the rating per site so it doesn't have to be re-analyzed for every user. It re-checks the policy periodically and re-rates it if the text changes. Your IP address is used, and kept for about a day, only to rate-limit requests and prevent abuse.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — the backend sends the public privacy policy text to Anthropic's Claude model to produce the rating. Anthropic processes this request under its own API terms; the text sent is the website's public privacy policy, not your personal data.

Stored on your device

A short-lived, in-memory record of the current tab's rating is kept so the popup can display it, and is cleared when the tab navigates away.

The extension never records which sites you type your phone number into, and never reads or stores the number itself.

Privacy Shield Supporter (paid)

Subscribers receive a licence key, stored in the extension's local storage. The extension sends it with rating requests so the backend knows it may check sites that haven't been rated yet. Uninstalling the extension deletes the key from your device.

Payments are handled by Stripe. The backend stores your licence key together with your Stripe subscription and customer IDs, and checks with Stripe whether the subscription is still active. Card details go only to Stripe and are never seen by Privacy Shield.

Backend cache

On the backend, only the computed rating for each site (green/yellow/orange/red plus the policy sentences supporting it) is cached, keyed by the site's domain; this cache holds no information about who requested it.

"Report an issue" feature

If you click "Report an issue" in the popup, the extension prepares a pre-filled email (page URL, whether a form/privacy link was detected, and the last rating shown) and opens it in your own email client. Nothing is sent automatically — you see the full contents and choose whether to send it, exactly like any other email you write.
