What Privacy Shield does

When a web page asks for your phone number, Privacy Shield reads that site's public privacy policy and shows a rating (red, yellow, green, or "couldn't check") describing whether your details may be sold or shared with companies that will contact you.

Ratings are guidance, not advice

  • Ratings are produced automatically, with AI, from the text of each site's privacy policy. They can be wrong or out of date, and sites can change their policies or practices at any time.
  • A rating describes what a site's policy says, not what the site actually does. It isn't legal advice, and it's no guarantee that you won't be contacted.
  • You decide whether to give a site your details. Always read a site's own privacy policy if anything matters to you.
